# Repository instructions

`AGENTS.md` is the canonical repository-wide instruction file. Read and follow it
before making changes.

Product truth lives in `README.md` and the linked documents under `docs/`.
Implementation status and priority live in `docs/ROADMAP.md`; engineering and
release gates live in `docs/STANDARDS.md` and `docs/VERIFY.md`. Keep durable facts
in those tracked files and keep local working notes inside ignored `.agent/`.
